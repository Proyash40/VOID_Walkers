Help & FAQ

Answers to the questions we get asked most about the team and how we operate.

Void Walkers is a tech community that spans every domain of computer science and engineering — hackathons, RoboWar, RoboRace, drones, machines and hardware, AI, cybersecurity, competitive coding, design and research. We build projects, compete in events and learn from each other.

Open the Join us page, pick the domains you are interested in, attach your CV and tell us why you want to join. The relevant department head reviews every application and we reply by email.

No fixed cutoff. We care more about curiosity and effort than existing skills. Beginners are paired with someone experienced in the domain they pick.

The Events page lists what we are organising and where we are competing next. Past events, with posters and reviews, live in the Hall of Fame.

Anyone who was there can send a review from an event's page. It reaches our inbox first and is added to the site once the team approves it, so the page stays free of spam.

Email off.voidwalker@gmail.com, message us on Instagram, or find us on the Void Walkers Discord. The contact page form lands in the same inbox.
