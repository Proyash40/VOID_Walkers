Help & FAQ

Answers to the questions we get asked most about the team and how we operate.

Void Walkers is a Capture The Flag team that competes in security challenges spanning web exploitation, binary exploitation, reverse engineering, cryptography, forensics and OSINT. We compete in both Jeopardy-style and Attack-Defense formats, online and on-site.

We open recruitment ahead of major competition seasons. The fastest way in is to reach out through the contact page with a short summary of your background and any write-ups or profiles you'd like to share — CTFtime, GitHub, or otherwise.

There's no strict cutoff. We value depth in at least one category — web, pwn, reversing, crypto, or forensics — over broad, shallow experience. New members typically pair with a category lead during their first few events.

The Database page tracks every event we've registered for or competed in, along with format, standing, and points where applicable.

Members regularly publish write-ups for the challenges they solve, usually linked from their individual profiles on the home page. We ask that flags and unreleased challenge details are withheld until organizers close the scoreboard.

Use the contact form and describe what you saw, including the page and roughly when it happened. Screenshots help.
