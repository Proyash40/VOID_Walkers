Community management hierarchy

How Void Walkers is organized, from founding leadership down to new members — and the access and responsibility that comes with each level.

Founder / Executive

Vision, strategy & overall leadership

Community Coordinators

Community growth, outreach, engagement, cross-team coordination & experience

Management (Core Team)

Strategic management, operations & governance

Operations Manager

Oversees daily operations, systems & execution

Finance & Treasurer

Handles budgeting, funds, resources & financial planning

HR & Talent Manager

Recruitment, training, well-being & internal culture

Strategy & Planning Manager

Planning, roadmaps, initiatives & long-term strategy

Legal & Compliance Manager

Policies, compliance, risk management & governance

Department Heads (Domain Leads)

Lead their respective domains & manage teams

Role progression ladder

How team members (non-core) move up, based on tenure, engagement and contribution.

1

Initiator

Just joined, learning & exploring

2

Explorer

Exploring actively, participating

3

Challenger

Taking challenges, contributing

4

Operator

Reliable & consistent, high engagement

5

Specialist

Skilled & proficient, mentors others

6

Elite

Exceptional impact, trusted member

7

Vanguard

Leadership impact, top contributor

Core Member

Trusted core — eligible for leadership roles

Note: time in server, engagement, contribution, skills & behavior are all evaluated for level-ups.

Important notes

  • Department channels are managed by respective Team Leads.
  • Event-specific channels are created & archived as needed.
  • Security & privacy are maintained at every level.
  • This hierarchy scales with the growth of the community.
  • Promotions are based on merit & contribution.
